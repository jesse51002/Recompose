# 06 — Restaurant

**Design-demand score 19/25** — Brand-load 4 · Variety 4 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 4 · Pay 2

Restaurants are the most visually opinionated small businesses that exist, and the ordering app is a conversion surface — so design carries both an identity argument and a revenue argument, which is rare this far down the list.

**The catch.** Money. Margins are thin, platform pricing sits around $149–179/mo, and the tenant most likely to care about its look is a single location that cannot fund a redesign. ChowNow, BentoBox and Popmenu all gate branding behind a tier.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Owner.com, Craver
