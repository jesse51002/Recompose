# 20 — Education

**Design-demand score 9/25** — Brand-load 2 · Variety 2 · Permission 2 · End-user stakes 1 · Pay 2

District identity exists — mascot, colours, a specific institutional formality — but the app is compliance-shaped infrastructure that parents and students use under obligation rather than by choice. Accessibility mandates are real and enforced, procurement is slow and committee-driven, and the deliverable is a communications utility.

**The catch.** ParentSquare (20M+ students) and PowerSchool (18,000+ customers, 60M+ students) are no-shell at very large scale — making this the second-clearest case of excellent addressability with no design demand underneath it.
