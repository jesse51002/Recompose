# 08 — Events

**Design-demand score 19/25** — Brand-load 4 · Variety 5 · Permission 4 · End-user stakes 3 · Pay 3

Structurally the most interesting entry in the ranking, for a reason the score does not capture. Recompose sells **designs, not tenants** — and events is the only vertical where one tenant needs a *new* design repeatedly, because every conference is a fresh brand campaign with its own identity. A 4,000-organiser platform running three events a year each is 12,000 designs annually, not 4,000: the highest design-per-tenant multiplier available anywhere in the list, and the cleanest fit to the unit of value.

**The catch.** The model works hard against it. Only EventMobi is no-shell; Cvent, Bizzabo and Swapcard all sit branded apps behind an enterprise tier, which is exactly where [[Concepts/Business/Pricing/Tier_Unlock_ROI|Tier_Unlock_ROI]] has to do all the work.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** EventMobi
