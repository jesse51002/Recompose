# 16 — Banking & Fintech

**Design-demand score 11/25** — Brand-load 2 · Variety 1 · Permission 1 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 5

The sharpest contradiction in the list: **highest ability to pay, lowest willingness to want.** Fintech has the cleanest denominators in the vault (~4,300 US credit unions, ~99% on vendors, a four-name vendor list) and all five platforms are no-shell, which is why Q2, Banno and Alkami sit on the current shortlist. But visual sameness here is a deliberate product decision rather than an unmet need: WCAG enforcement is baked into Alkami's Theme Builder, Banno ships ADA-compliant colour outputs, SOC 2 review runs per change, and Alkami's 240+ FI apps share visual DNA **on purpose** because trust signals reward consistency, not novelty. Every banking AI announcement 2024–2026 is end-user AI, not design AI.

**The catch.** Deep visual differentiation multiplies compliance review surface area linearly with theme count. The product's core promise is a **cost** to this buyer. Source: `Research/2026-05-01-ai-brand-competitor-analysis.md:45`, `Design_vs_Feature_Work.md:85`.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Alkami, Q2 Holdings, Banno (Jack Henry), Bankjoy, Narmi
