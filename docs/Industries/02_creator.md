# 02 — Creator

**Design-demand score 22/25** — Brand-load 5 · Variety 5 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 4 · Pay 3

The creator *is* the brand — there is no business underneath the personal identity — so a templated app reads as a franchise of somebody else's product, which is precisely what a creator is selling against. Variety is as high as it gets, because two creators looking alike damages both. The demand is already priced: Mighty Pro and Circle sell a branded app at an estimated $28–33k/yr, and Passion.io's DFY tier at $10–20k setup.

**The catch.** The model. Mighty Networks, Circle and Kajabi are all shell+upgrade, where measured attach is 0.1–3%, so the addressable app count is a small fraction of the tenant count. Only Passion.io (15,000+ apps launched) and Disciple Media provision every tenant an app.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Passion.io, Disciple Media
