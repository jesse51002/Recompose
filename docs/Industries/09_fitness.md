# 09 — Fitness & Wellness

**Design-demand score 18/25** — Brand-load 4 · Variety 4 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 3 · Pay 2

Boutique studios are heavily brand-conscious and visually heterogeneous, and this is the vertical with the deepest founder fluency and the largest platform count in the list.

**The catch.** It also carries the most direct **negative** evidence in the vault: five phases of gym-facing product produced no sale, and the depth-of-customization tiers were never validated against a paying owner. Nine of twelve platforms are shell+upgrade, and base prices of $30–139/mo describe the budget accurately.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** ABC Glofox, EGYM, Exercise.com
