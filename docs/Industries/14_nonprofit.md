# 14 — Nonprofits & Associations

**Design-demand score 14/25** — Brand-load 3 · Variety 3 · Permission 4 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 2

Associations care about identity — it is most of what a membership organisation sells — but the money and the decision-making are conservative, procurement is committee-driven, and the app is a members-only utility that never competes for attention.

**The catch.** Glue Up is the one no-shell platform and it bundles two free apps into a ~$1,500–2,500/yr plan, which sets the ceiling on what a design is worth here.
