# 07 — Salon & Spa

**Design-demand score 19/25** — Brand-load 5 · Variety 4 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 3 · Pay 2

An aesthetics business selling aesthetics through an app that looks like every other salon's is a visible self-contradiction, and this is the vertical where the tenant is most likely to hold an actual point of view about type and colour. Zenoti serves multi-location chains at ~$400/mo+, which is where the money in the vertical sits.

**The catch.** The long tail prices the work honestly and low: GlossGenius starts around $24/mo, and Vagaro sells its design package at **$499 one-time** — the market stating what it currently believes this deliverable is worth.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Zenoti, Phorest
