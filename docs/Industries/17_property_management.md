# 17 — Property Management

**Design-demand score 10/25** — Brand-load 1 · Variety 2 · Permission 3 · End-user stakes 1 · Pay 3

Perfect addressability and almost nothing to sell. All six platforms are no-shell at enormous scale — AppFolio at 7.8M units under management, RealPage at 24M+ — and a resident does not choose their building's app. They are assigned it, use it to pay rent and file maintenance tickets, and would not notice a redesign. A property manager's brand does no work on a captive audience.

**The catch.** This is the clearest case in the list of the **addressability filter alone producing a bad target**. Passing the no-shell test is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** AppFolio, Entrata, RealPage, Buildium, Yardi/RentCafe, Frontsteps
