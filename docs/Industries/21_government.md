# 21 — Government

**Design-demand score 7/25** — Brand-load 1 · Variety 2 · Permission 1 · End-user stakes 1 · Pay 2

The floor of the ranking, for structural reasons that will not move. Section 508 and WCAG compliance are legal requirements rather than preferences, procurement actively penalises novelty, and a citizen service app is deliberately built to look like a citizen service app — legibility and institutional trust are the goals, and visual distinctiveness works against both.

**The catch.** CivicPlus is no-shell across 12,000+ customers. Every property that makes this vertical addressable also makes it unsellable.
