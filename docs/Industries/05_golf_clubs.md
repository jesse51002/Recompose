# 05 — Golf & Clubs

**Design-demand score 20/25** — Brand-load 5 · Variety 4 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 4

The most underrated entry in the ranking. A private club sells exclusivity, and exclusivity is signalled almost entirely through visual restraint, heritage marks and material quality — so a generic app does not merely fail to help, it actively undercuts the product a member pays dues for. Crests, club colours and typographic tradition are non-negotiable and unique per club, and clubs have money. Both major platforms are no-shell.

**The catch.** Market size. Roughly 10–15k clubs across the addressable set, and stakes are low in the narrow sense: members are already members and are not shopping.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Clubessential, Jonas Club
