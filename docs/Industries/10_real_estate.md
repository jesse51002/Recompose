# 10 — Real Estate

**Design-demand score 18/25** — Brand-load 4 · Variety 4 · Permission 4 · End-user stakes 3 · Pay 3

Agent personal branding is a real and heavily-funded market — Luxury Presence raised a $37M Series C in January 2026 essentially on the premise that an agent's presentation is their differentiator, and the luxury segment competes on visual sophistication in a way most verticals do not. Variety demand is high because every agent wants to look like their own practice rather than their brokerage.

**The catch.** Unit size. The tenant is an individual, platforms count agents in the hundreds of thousands (Inside Real Estate 250–500k), and only HomeStack is no-shell. The design demand is real; the per-design willingness to pay is not.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** HomeStack
