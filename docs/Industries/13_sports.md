# 13 — Sports

**Design-demand score 15/25** — Brand-load 4 · Variety 3 · Permission 4 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 2

Team colours and crests are as close to sacred as brand identity gets, and no organisation will accept them being approximated. But that demand is almost entirely satisfied by **logo-plus-colour** — which is the reskin every platform already ships and the exact thing this company exists to escape. Structural variety demand is low: a roster, a schedule and a stats page look the same everywhere, so [[Concepts/Product/Mechanisms/Full_Visual_Redesign|Full_Visual_Redesign]] has little to act on that a theme picker does not already cover.

**The catch.** None of the six platforms is no-shell; TeamSnap, SportsEngine and Hudl all gate branding behind a tier.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** — none
