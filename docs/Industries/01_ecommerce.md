# 01 — Ecommerce

**Design-demand score 24/25** — Brand-load 5 · Variety 5 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 5 · Pay 4

The app **is** the storefront, so the tenant's visual identity is not decoration applied to the product — it is the product, and a DTC brand arrives already holding a design system and a merchandising point of view it needs expressed. Variety is maximal because two brands sharing a look is a commercial problem for both of them. It is also the only vertical in the list where design ROI is **measurable rather than argued**: conversion rate moves, the tenant already watches it, and that makes this the one place T19 — whether attach rate responds to design quality at all — can be answered with a number instead of a story.

**The catch.** These tenants are already the self-selected 1.73% who bought an app at all, and Tapcart-class builders ship drag-drop layout editors — so the layout half of the mechanism competes with something the tenant already has, against a $50M-funded incumbent well placed to build it.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Tapcart, Plobal Apps, Vajro (Superfans), MageNative, Shopney, JMango360
