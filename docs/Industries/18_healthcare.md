# 18 — Healthcare

**Design-demand score 10/25** — Brand-load 2 · Variety 2 · Permission 1 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 3

Clinical interfaces are conservative on purpose. Patients want to find their appointment and their results; accessibility requirements are hard, HIPAA shapes what can be on screen, and the visual conventions that read as *trustworthy medical* are narrow and widely shared. The vault's own research pairs healthcare with banking as verticals likely to **actively resist** deep visual differentiation.

**The catch.** One pocket is genuinely different: Healthie's coaching- and wellness-adjacent tenants are closer to the creator vertical than to a health system, and brand does real work for them.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Healthie, Luma Health, Nudge Coach
