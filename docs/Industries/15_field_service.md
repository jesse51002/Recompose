# 15 — Field Service

**Design-demand score 12/25** — Brand-load 2 · Variety 2 · Permission 4 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 2

The trade's brand lives on a truck wrap and a yard sign, not in a client hub the homeowner opens twice per job. Design carries almost no commercial weight, and variety demand is low because a scheduling-and-invoice flow is the same everywhere.

**The catch.** Large, well-funded platforms — ServiceTitan (NASDAQ: TTAN), Housecall Pro, Jobber (100,000+ customers) — with nothing here to sell them. None is no-shell.
