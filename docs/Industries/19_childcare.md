# 19 — Childcare

**Design-demand score 10/25** — Brand-load 2 · Variety 2 · Permission 3 · End-user stakes 1 · Pay 2

Parent-facing utility software: daily reports, photos, sign-in, invoicing. A centre's brand matters at enrolment, which happens in person or on a website — not in the app the parent uses every day afterward. Variety demand is low and budgets are small.

**The catch.** None of Brightwheel, Procare or Lillio (HiMama) is no-shell, so even the low demand is gated behind a tier.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** — none
