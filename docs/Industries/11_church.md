# 11 — Church & Faith

**Design-demand score 16/25** — Brand-load 3 · Variety 4 · Permission 5 · End-user stakes 2 · Pay 2

Mid-pack on design demand and first in the entire list on addressability — and it matters which leg the Subsplash thesis is standing on. Churches are strongly identity-driven and visually varied across denomination and congregation age, with no regulatory pressure toward sameness. But the identity is **cultural rather than commercial**: nobody chooses a church by its app, so [[Concepts/Business/Strategy/Brand_Is_Product|Brand_Is_Product]] gets no support here, and budgets are thin.

**The catch.** The case for Subsplash is 20,000+ tenants each already holding an app, plus a former customer on record leaving over customization limits. It is a volume-and-addressability case, not a design-demand case.

**No-shell platforms in this vertical:** Subsplash, Pushpay
