# 03 — Hotels

**Design-demand score 21/25** — Brand-load 5 · Variety 5 · Permission 4 · End-user stakes 3 · Pay 4

Hospitality sells a feeling, and the visual distance between a boutique property and a budget chain is the entire pricing argument — a guest app that looks like every other guest app quietly contradicts a $600 room rate. Variety is genuinely maximal here because the tenant set spans the widest aesthetic range of any vertical in the list, from heritage luxury to hostel. Budgets are real and the buyer is used to paying for brand work.

**The catch.** Stakes are what hold the score down: the guest downloads the app *after* booking, so the look never enters the purchase decision and the platform cannot point at a deal it lost on it.
