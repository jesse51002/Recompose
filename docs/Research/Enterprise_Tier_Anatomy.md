# What white-label enterprise tiers actually sell

Concepts touched: [[Tier_Unlock_ROI]], [[Full_Visual_Redesign]], [[AI_Design_Compression]].

A record of what is included in the premium "branded app" tier at the two platforms that document it most fully — Mighty Pro and Circle Plus — what the dedicated human attached to that tier does, and where the design work sits inside it. Compiled 2026-08-02 from vendor pages, a live job posting, and third-party pricing write-ups. Sources are linked inline.

---

## The tier contents

### Mighty Pro

[Mighty Pro's own services page](https://www.mightynetworks.com/pro/services) lists six deliverables. Design is one of them.

1. **Migration** — "We'll bring over everything you need to make your new home feel like home." Named: **members, courses & content, payments.**
2. **Business strategy** — "With the latest insights across **450+ 6-and-7-figure businesses**, we'll create a **Community Design™ Plan** you can run with informed by exactly what works." Deliverables listed: data-driven engagement tactics, a custom member onboarding experience, a personalized launch plan.
3. **Setup & design** — network setup with initial spaces, network and app-store branding, feature and automation configuration.
4. **Design services** — "Our world-class designers will create **splash screens, app icons, App Store & Google Play listing screens, and community graphics**."
5. **Mighty Pro Membership** — exclusive platform trainings and previews, weekly strategic mastermind.
6. **Ongoing** — Mighty Pro Insights™, automated reporting to the customer's team.

Stated launch timeline: **4–6 weeks** on the services page; **3–4 weeks** in the job posting and third-party write-ups.

### Circle Plus

Per [Circle's Plus breakdown](https://sellcoursesonline.com/circle-plus) and [SchoolMaker's pricing analysis](https://www.schoolmaker.com/blog/circle-so-pricing) — Circle does not publish this on its own site:

- Branded iOS and Android apps, **built, published, maintained and updated** by the Circle team
- White-labeling "every inch of the platform, including logos, colors, push notifications, and email notifications"
- **Course and payment migration** performed by Circle's team
- Higher limits — spaces, moderators, administrators, streaming hours
- **Design Studio** — "Circle's design team creates marketing screenshots, splash screens, and app icons for you"
- VIP support — dedicated strategist, office hours, "support calls on a regular basis even after the apps are live"
- Reported additions: AI Agents (up to 10), custom SSO via SAML/OAuth, sandbox community, dedicated CSM, transaction fee reduced to 0.5%, 10,000 Email Hub contacts

Stated launch timeline: ~4 weeks.

## What the dedicated human does

From the live [Community Strategist posting at Mighty Networks](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/mightynetworks/jobs/8523950002). The listed responsibilities are business-operations work, not product or design work:

- "Serve as a strategic partner to new Mighty Pro Hosts during their onboarding phase as they set up their Network in the way that leads to **maximum member adoption, engagement, and retention**"
- "Facilitate impactful **workshops** with Hosts and their teams that establish strong foundations in **community design, content cadence, and engagement strategy**"
- "Deliver targeted **training** on feature utilization, member onboarding flows, and tactical programming to drive adoption, activation, and retention"
- "Provide creative input and feedback on brand elements like **landing pages, welcome experiences, email sequences, and network messaging**"
- "Guide new Mighty Pro customers through onboarding, community design strategy, custom mobile app development, and launch"
- "Scale success through **repeatable frameworks**"

Two things are visible in that list. The engagement is **productized** — "Community Design™" is a trademarked methodology and the posting describes repeatable frameworks, not bespoke consulting. And the asset being sold is **benchmark data**: the pattern library from 450+ six- and seven-figure community businesses, which the customer has no other way to acquire.

The three risks the engagement covers are migration (members, courses and payments moving without breaking), the launch itself (a one-time event with the customer's existing audience), and post-launch adoption.

Circle's equivalent role is described only as a "dedicated Circle strategist" with office hours and regular calls; no responsibility list is published.

## Where the design work sits

At both platforms the design service is enumerated separately from the strategist's work, and its scope is:

| Platform | Design deliverables |
|---|---|
| Mighty Pro | Splash screens, app icons, App Store and Google Play listing screens, community graphics |
| Circle Plus | Marketing screenshots, splash screens, app icons |

Neither list includes in-app screens, layout, illustration, iconography beyond the app icon, or a typographic system. This is consistent with the cross-platform survey in `Research/AIBrandCopetitiorAnaylsisv2.md:17–23`, which found custom per-customer illustration, iconography and motion at ~0% across ~80 platforms in 14 verticals, and custom typographic systems at <5%.

## Prices, and how well sourced they are

Neither platform publishes enterprise-tier pricing. [Circle's pricing page](https://circle.so/pricing) and [Mighty Networks' pricing page](https://www.mightynetworks.com/pricing) both gate the tier behind contact-sales, so every figure in circulation is third-party.

| Platform | Figure in circulation | Source quality |
|---|---|---|
| Circle Plus | "expected cost is **$30,000/year**," $7,500/quarter option | Third-party estimate. The word used is *expected*, not quoted |
| Circle Plus | customers "quoted anywhere from **$10,000 to $30,000 per month**" | Third-party, uncited. Conflicts with the above by ~12× |
| Mighty Pro | **$1,167+/mo** (May 2026 research) | Third-party, now stale |
| Mighty Pro | **$3,000–5,000+/month** | Third-party estimate |
| Mighty Pro | **$28,000–30,000/year** | Third-party estimate |

No customer statement, quote document or invoice appears in any source. `YC_Application_F26.md` already instructs using "$1,000+/month" rather than a precise figure; the same caution applies to every row above.

## The standalone price of branded-app design

Two platforms sell the branded-app design work unbundled, which is the only direct market pricing of the asset production itself:

- **ABC Trainerize** — Custom Branded App, **one-time ~$99–$169**, on top of a ~$250/mo subscription
- **Vagaro** — design package, **$499 one-time** (logo, brand kit, splash images, app icons); custom website design ~$499 US/CA/AU, £399 GBP

Both figures are for the same category of deliverable the enterprise tiers list — splash, icons, store assets, brand kit. The enterprise-tier price is therefore not principally a price for design asset production.

## What is not established

- The number of tenants on any platform's branded-app tier. Every revenue implication depends on this figure and no source contains it.
- What the design service costs the platform to run. Circle's design department is reported at ~4 people out of ~160, but that team is described as Circle's internal *marketing* design function, so the tenant-facing share is unknown. No headcount is published for Vagaro's Design Services Team, Mindbody's internal design team, or Subsplash's onboarding staff.
- Circle Plus's full tier contents from a first-party source.
- Any verified enterprise-tier price.
