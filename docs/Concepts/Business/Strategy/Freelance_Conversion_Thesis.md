# Freelance Conversion Thesis

A strategic filter for vertical selection: target the verticals where SMBs or individuals currently pay freelancers $10k-$50k+ for custom apps, because that population has explicit dollar-validated willingness to pay for customization (they rejected templated white-label and chose to pay extra for custom). Offer them an AI-customized alternative at SaaS pricing — same outcome, different cost structure.

This frame is materially different from "biggest white-label market." White-label customers already accepted generic; the thesis doesn't unlock new value for them. Freelance custom-build customers explicitly *rejected* generic and are getting overcharged because design used to be expensive labor. They are the population the thesis is for. As an applied filter: restaurants do NOT fit (converged on white-label years ago, freelance custom-build market is thin in this category). The creator economy fits well — coaches, course creators, community operators, and top-tier content creators all routinely commission custom builds at the relevant price tier.
