# Brand Is Product

The dynamic where a customer's brand identity is not separable from the product they sell — the brand IS the product. Most acute in personal-brand businesses (coaches, content creators, course creators, community operators) where the customer's audience buys *because of who the seller is*, not because of generic features.

For these customers, brand-coherence in every digital surface is a primary commercial concern, not a vanity feature. The implication for the architecture thesis: customers in brand-is-product verticals are the most willing to pay for deep design customization because generic-looking digital surfaces actively undermine their commercial position. A coach's branded app reinforces the coach's identity 7-15 times a week; a generic templated app dilutes it. This is the load-bearing customer-side argument for why the wedge resonates in creator-economy verticals more than in operationally-driven ones (restaurants, healthcare, construction).
