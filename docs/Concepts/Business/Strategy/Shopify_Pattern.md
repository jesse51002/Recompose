# Shopify Pattern

The strategic move of building a rock-solid fixed structure for a vertical-specific software category and exposing customization as a deep theming layer on top, so that customers compete on identity rather than on whether the structure works. Named after Shopify, which used this move to win ecommerce: every Shopify merchant gets the same checkout, catalog, and inventory engine, but they compete on theme and brand expression on top.

The pattern's defining property is that *structure is taken off the table* — every customer's structure works equally well, freeing the customer's commercial energy to be spent on the part their own customers actually feel (the brand experience). The architecture thesis applies this pattern to verticals other than ecommerce. The [[AI_Design_Compression]] unlock is what makes deep theming affordable at SaaS prices for verticals that were previously locked into templated white-label.
