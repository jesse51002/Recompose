# AI Design Compression

The post-2024 cost compression of producing distinct visual design — illustration sets, iconography, typography pairings, motion presets, copy tone profiles — from weeks of human labor to hours of AI-driven generation. Pre-AI cost of per-customer custom design was $5k-$15k via freelance designers or $20k-$50k via agencies; post-AI, the marginal cost drops to hours of operator/AI tool time per customer.

This compression is the load-bearing technical premise behind the architecture thesis. It is the reason fixed-structure-plus-deep-customization is viable at SaaS prices in 2026 and was not viable in 2024. Without this compression, the per-customer custom-design cost exceeds years of revenue at the SaaS price point and the architecture cannot ship economically.
